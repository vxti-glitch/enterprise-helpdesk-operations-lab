# KB011 - Department transfer and access change

> **SIMULATED KNOWLEDGE ARTICLE.** Names, approvals, systems, and outcomes are fictional.

## Purpose

Process an approved department-transfer access change without carrying forward access that no longer has a business need.

## Tier 1 checklist

1. Verify the approved request, effective date, manager, and destination department.
2. Identify baseline access to retain, access to remove, and any resolver-group task.
3. Use the approved synthetic group list; do not infer entitlement from a job title.
4. Record the request, approval, action, validation, and plain-language user update.
5. Escalate mailbox, tenant, or application-owner work with the complete handoff package.

## Validation

Confirm the user can complete the approved destination workflow and that explicitly removed access no longer appears in the fictional lab record.
